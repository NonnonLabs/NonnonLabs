# NONNON

NONNON AI is a frontier research lab building deep reasoning models for knowledge professionals. Initially trained on Swiss and European Human Rights Convention law, we have expanded to the legal systems set out on our website.

Our first public model is NONNON-small for law. In our in-house evaluations, the model consistently scores above 93% on the LegalBench benchmark; see details below.

- Public reproduction recipe: https://github.com/NonnonLabs/nonnon-small-legalbench
- Python SDK: https://github.com/NonnonLabs/nonnon-sdk-python
- Model cards: https://github.com/NonnonLabs/model-cards
- Hugging Face model card: https://huggingface.co/NonnonLabs/nonnon-small
- Demo placeholder: https://huggingface.co/spaces/NonnonLabs/nonnon-small-demo

Evaluation tokens are available for legitimate benchmarking. Email labs@nonnon.ai.

NONNON-medium consistently scores 100% on this benchmark. Our in-house evaluations also score highly on benchmaks for Swiss, UK, EU, and European Convention on Human Rights law.
